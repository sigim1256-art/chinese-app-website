Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. You can use the app with complete peace of mind knowing that your activity remains entirely private.

2. 100% Offline Functionality

The app operates completely offline. It does not communicate with any external servers. This means there is no data transmission, no telemetrics, and no user tracking of any kind.

3. Local Storage

Any progress you make within the app (such as learning milestones) is saved locally on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is never uploaded to the cloud by the app itself.
